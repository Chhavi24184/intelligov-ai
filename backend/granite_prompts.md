# IntelliGov AI - IBM Granite Prompt Ideas

Use these prompts to test IBM Granite as the conversational layer for scheme discovery, eligibility checks, recommendations, RAG-grounded answers, and Explainable AI responses.

1. I am a small farmer from Uttar Pradesh. Which government schemes can help me with income support, crop insurance, and agricultural loans?
2. I am a 24-year-old student from a low-income family. Find scholarships that I may be eligible for.
3. I need health insurance for my family. Explain whether Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY may be suitable for me.
4. I want to start a tailoring business from home. Compare PM Mudra Yojana and PMEGP for me.
5. I am a street vendor and need money to expand my food cart. What government support should I explore?
6. I am a rural woman who wants skill training and employment. Recommend relevant schemes and tell me what to do first.
7. I am a 31-year-old construction worker in the unorganised sector. Which pension and insurance schemes may suit me?
8. I want to build a house but my family income is low. Check whether PM Awas Yojana may be relevant.
9. My crop was damaged due to floods. Explain how Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana can help and list the documents I may need.
10. I am a carpenter. Can I apply for PM Vishwakarma? Explain the benefits in simple language.
11. My daughter is seven years old. How can I use Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana for her future savings?
12. I have completed Class 12 and want to study engineering. Which scholarship opportunities should I check?
13. I left school after Class 10. Recommend free skill-development programmes that can help me get a job.
14. I want to install rooftop solar panels at my home. Explain PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana and the likely application steps.
15. I have Aadhaar and a bank account but no income certificate. Which schemes can I explore now, and which document should I obtain next?
16. Create a personalised opportunity list for a 22-year-old unemployed graduate from a rural area.
17. I am a woman from the OBC category who wants to start a small bakery. What loan, training, and scholarship options may be relevant?
18. I am a tenant farmer and do not own land. Can I access agricultural credit, crop insurance, or any other farmer benefits?
19. Explain why you recommended PM Kaushal Vikas Yojana to me based on my profile.
20. Compare Atal Pension Yojana, PMJJBY, and PMSBY in a simple table.
21. I am a person with a disability and have no regular income. Identify possible pension, healthcare, and education-support schemes.
22. I am a widow with two children. What government benefits should I check for financial support, housing, education, and health?
23. I need to know the exact documents required before applying for PM Mudra Yojana.
24. Which schemes am I likely to become eligible for after completing a digital-marketing skill course?
25. Give me a step-by-step application checklist for PM Kisan Samman Nidhi.
26. I am eligible for more than one scheme. Rank them by urgency, financial benefit, and ease of application.
27. Explain this scheme in Hindi and use simple words: Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana.
28. I am a young woman from a village who wants an apprenticeship in healthcare or computer work. What should I explore?
29. What information is missing from my profile before you can check my eligibility accurately?
30. Use only the retrieved government-scheme data. If information is missing or uncertain, clearly say that you cannot confirm it and tell me where to verify it.
